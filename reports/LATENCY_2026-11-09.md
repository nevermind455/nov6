Base: `8d8fd42` on `nevermind455/nov6`. Branch:

## Review of the six proposed improvements

| Area | Existing behavior and resulting change |
| --- | --- |
| Pre-signing | Size and cap are dynamic and salt/timestamp are signature-bound. Cache immutable signing data and remove repeat parsing/allocation; do not substitute speculative pre-signed orders. |
| Warm connections | The order sender already reuses HTTP/2/TLS, with `/time` probes and HTTP/2 PINGs. Add verified TCP_NODELAY/SO_KEEPALIVE and expose cached connection generation/readiness. |
| WebSocket prices | Coinbase/market WebSockets already drive decisions directly. Background REST discovery, recovery and reconciliation remain unchanged. |
| Native hot path | Optimize digest/order construction without changing signed bytes or removing pre-send checks, fsync or final guards. |
| VPS region | Provide a native read-only probe for AMS3 and actual candidate hosts. No regional measurements or relocation are claimed from this workspace. |
| Timestamp attribution | Add true dispatch-entry T1, preserve full write/response boundaries, record trade receipt source/time and keep venue timestamps raw. Extend missed-order diagnostics without conflating polling with WebSocket receipt. |

Implementation covers `src/crypto.cpp`, the native REST/WebSocket/HTTP2
transports, `include/sniper/network.hpp`, runtime timing/evidence capture,
`tools/report/miss_report.hpp`, the new `sniper-net-probe` CLI, and appropriate
native/CLI regressions and benchmarks. The
[usage and timing guide](../docs/EXECUTION_LATENCY.md) describes exact fields
and the command to run on AMS3.

## Measured local signing components

The same native microbenchmarks ran before and after the crypto changes in
the same Ubuntu 24.04/GCC 13 environment with normal `-O2` compilation and
three reported logical CPUs. Each case used seven repetitions with a
0.5-second minimum per repetition. Values below are medians of CPU time,
converted from the raw nanosecond output.

| Component | Before, µs | After, µs |
| --- | ---: | ---: |
| `OrderDigest` | 23.985 | 9.873 |
| `BuildOrder` | 65.989 | 46.773 |
| `Signing` (ECDSA only) | 30.299 | 29.139 |

`BuildOrder` includes fresh salt/timestamp generation, digest, ECDSA and
serialization. The ECDSA algorithm was not changed; its small variation
illustrates run-to-run drift. Before/after batches were not randomized or
interleaved, and their reported load averages differed. The benchmark library
reported a **DEBUG build warning** even though the bot's normal target uses
`-O2`; do not present these absolute figures as production VPS latency.

The measured component reductions support removing repeat digest preparation
work. They do not establish an end-to-end execution speedup, a particular
network improvement, a better fill rate or profitability. No WAN percentage
improvement is claimed for the socket options.

Raw benchmark artifacts: [`latency-2026-11-09/crypto-before.json`](latency-2026-11-09/crypto-before.json)
and [`crypto-after.json`](latency-2026-11-09/crypto-after.json).

## What the new timing evidence can establish

T1 now separates decision work (`T0→T1`) from live pre-send checks (`T1→T2`).
Existing T2–T6 retain their boundaries. T5 is complete TLS/socket-write
completion, not physical NIC transmission. T6 is application response-callback
entry, not a venue execution timestamp. No duration is invented when stamps
are absent or reversed.

`network_and_ack` combines network and venue response time. `/time` probe RTT
also includes client queuing and endpoint processing; subtracting the two
does not identify a proven exchange-imposed delay. Trade confirmation receipt
is reported separately for WebSocket and REST reconciliation, joined only to
the order's dispatch run. Raw venue time fields remain uninterpreted.

Legacy journals without T1 retain their older T0-to-T2 aggregate and are
identified as including checks. The legacy wall-clock
`send_to_first_fill_ms` remains labeled separately from the new monotonic,
source-specific confirmation-evidence measurements.
